About Gateway Visas

We built this company because visa applications shouldn't require a PhD in bureaucracy.

Our Story

Gateway Visas was founded on a simple observation: millions of people every year miss out on travel, work, and study opportunities because the visa process is confusing, time-consuming, and unnecessarily stressful.

We set out to change that. Our mission is to make visa processing as easy as booking a flight. No queues. No confusing forms. No waiting in government offices for hours.

Today, Gateway Visas serves clients from over 190 countries, handling every visa type imaginable — from tourist visas for dream holidays to work permits for global careers. Everything happens online. You apply from your phone. We handle the rest.

Why We Exist

Traditional visa services are slow, expensive, and opaque. You fill out forms you don't understand, wait weeks for responses, and pay fees without knowing what you're actually getting.

We cut through all of that. Our system is designed to be transparent, fast, and affordable. You know exactly what documents you need, exactly what it costs, and exactly when you'll hear back.

Whether you're a student heading to university, a professional attending a conference, a family visiting relatives, or a football fan chasing the World Cup — we make sure paperwork never stands between you and your destination.
