Frequently Asked Questions

How long does visa processing take?

It varies by country and visa type. Tourist visas typically take 2-4 weeks, while work visas can take 2-3 months. We provide accurate timelines during your consultation.

What documents do I need?

Generally: valid passport, photos, application form, proof of funds, travel itinerary, and supporting documents specific to your visa type. We send you a complete checklist after you apply.

Is my data secure?

Absolutely. All documents are transmitted via encrypted email and stored securely. We never share your information with third parties.

What if my visa is rejected?

We review applications thoroughly before submission to minimize rejection risk. If rejected, we help you understand the reason and reapply or appeal where possible.

Do you guarantee visa approval?

No visa service can guarantee approval — that decision rests with the issuing country's immigration authorities. However, our expertise significantly improves your chances.
